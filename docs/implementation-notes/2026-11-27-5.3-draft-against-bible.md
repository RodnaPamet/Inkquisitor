# 2026-11-27 — 5.3 Draft against bible (D3, D35)

**Commit:** uncommitted (branch `wip/5.3`, cut from `main` at `a3a3853`)

Phase 5's third subphase holds the manuscript against the canon. The nightly pass reads each scene whose text changed,
draws DRAFT facts from it through the Phase 4 fact rules, and compares them with the bible's facts: a mismatch is a
continuity finding citing the scene's sentence and the bible entry, which the author decides with one of three
choices. The same reading proposes which threads a scene plants or pays off. "Grill me on this scene" is a
scene-scoped interview whose answers fill the scene's CARD (goal, conflict, outcome, what changed), never its prose.
The owner's four decisions of 2026-11-27 are recorded as D35 and the 5.3 text is amended to match; their three answers
to the review (the deep check reading the draft as a per-book option, the night's share in halves, and "Recently
resolved") are D35's addendum, at the end of this note.

## Decisions

- **A prompt of its own.** The draft reading is `DRAFT_READ_PROMPT` (4.0's fact rules plus the thread links), with its
  own set, record and D11 gate, rather than a change to `EXTRACT_PROMPT`: 4.0's record and every source ingestion
  stay as they were, and the links need output the source extraction must not have.
- **Draft facts are never canon.** They are PENDING and undecided by CHECK, never embedded (the embedding trigger's
  WHEN), left out of the continuity discovery, the gap signals and the Facts page's proposals, and compared only
  against BIBLE facts — a confirmed source fact is an earlier draft, not the canon, and an explanation states no value.
  Rules only: the deep check is not run on drafts in 5.3.
- **A mismatch is a StoryFactProposal of basis DRAFT** (one table, as D27 asked), with statuses of its own held by
  CHECK and a partial unique index for "one open mismatch a bible fact, scene and claim"; KEPT and BIBLE_UPDATED block
  a re-raise, FIXED does not (a claim the author restores is raised again). A superseded draft fact is kept only while a
  mismatch rests on it, and views place it by the LIVE fact with the same claim key.
- **"Update the bible" is the author's save.** The decision is recorded only in the transaction that writes the
  author's version (never a decision alone), a save that changes nothing is refused (400 NOTHING_CHANGED), and a
  relation fact offers no update (its field cannot be named): "both true" or a fix instead.
- **The order within a night.** The continuity check (02:23) before the draft reading (03:07), both on one share: the
  canon's own check is never starved by the manuscript.
- **Eye colour.** The roadmap's own example needed a rule: APPEARANCE clashes only on one colour per named feature, from
  a closed list, with precision cases (true clashes and near misses) added to the rules' precision set.
- **The carrier element.** A question needs an element to be filed under (the interview's existing schema), so a scene
  question names its scene beside a carrier; a scene with nothing to carry it is refused, named, rather than guessed.
- **The frontier only.** A scene session runs the frontier generator alone over the card's four fields: the ladder,
  relations, sources and continuity generators ask about bible fields and would wander off the card.

## Limits

- Story time: a scene takes a place in the story order only when linked to exactly ONE placed plot beat; otherwise its
  facts carry only text times (their own, else the scene's), which decide an overlap only by equal words. A
  WHEREABOUTS or KNOWLEDGE mismatch therefore needs the scene on the plot board or a stated time.
- A piece the guard refuses yields nothing; when every piece of a scene is refused, the scene's earlier draft facts are
  kept (reading nothing is not evidence the claims are gone).
- The deep check runs over draft facts only for a book whose author turned that on (D35 addendum A, below); otherwise
  a mismatch the rules cannot see (synonyms, a role, a want) is not raised.
- (Superseded by D35 addendum B, below: the reading is now guaranteed half the share while it has work.) The order
  within a night protected the canon's check, not the reading: a workspace whose continuity check spent the whole
  nightly share every night stopped the draft reading NIGHTLY_CAP every night.
- An appearance's colour is read from the fact's whole statement, so "Wren looked into Ostrik's grey eyes" stated as a
  fact about Wren would be compared as Wren's (the extractor names one subject a fact; the rule cannot see whose eyes).
- A link proposal's quote is the scene's sentence when it was read; deciding it later does not re-check that the scene
  still says it.
- The Continuity page reads at most 1,000 confirmed links for the threads it shows and does not name a cut: past it a
  thread's pay-off may be unseen and the thread marked unpaid (the interview's generator, by contrast, decides nothing
  on a cut read).

## Review fixes

An independent review of the subphase found and fixed, each with a test that fails without it:

- **A failed read was taken as "unchanged".** `countFailure` records the failing text's key on the cursor, so the next
  night the same text matched `readTextKey` and took the details-only path: no call, the cursor set OK — a text whose
  read failed once was never read, and never reached `DRAFT_SCENE_FAILURES_MAX`. The details-only path now needs a
  cursor that READ its text (`cursorReadItsText`: OK / EMPTY / GUARD_REFUSED, no failure counted); a PASSED_OVER text
  stays passed over through a details-only save.
- **A guard-refused piece retracted its claims.** When some pieces of a changed scene were refused, the facts the
  refused pieces had held were superseded and their mismatches settled FIXED. A live draft fact whose quote still
  stands in a refused piece is now kept (reading nothing is not evidence it is gone).
- **Open mismatches outlived their bible fact.** A FIX_PENDING mismatch whose bible fact the author set aside, and any
  open one whose bible fact was retracted, stayed in the open list for ever (the re-read settled only against bible
  facts it read). Now 4.1's `settleAllForFact` settles a FIX_PENDING draft mismatch FIXED, and a re-read that read
  every live bible fact settles the rest (PENDING → INVALIDATED, FIX_PENDING → FIXED).
- **A clash settled by the clock could never be raised again.** 4.1's `StoryFactProposal_pair_key` ("one proposal a
  pair, ever") held draft pairs too, so a pair settled FIXED because the scene moved in story time, with the draft fact
  still live, was silently skipped when it moved back. The index now leaves basis DRAFT out (the open-mismatch index
  holds those).
- **A MANUSCRIPT version could name any scene.** The CHECK only required a scene id; a trigger
  (`bible_entry_version_manuscript_scene_in_book`) now refuses one that is not a scene of the version's own book.
- **Rule 6.** A scene every piece of which the guard refused, or whose text failed, counted as READ (the margin then
  said "no mismatches"): it is now passed over on the Continuity page and in the margin, never read with nothing found.
- **Eye colour precision.** Each feature compares only its own colours (red or white eyes are states), singular "eye"
  ("a black eye") and skin are not compared, a colour followed by "with" / "in" / "from" is a state or a light, and a
  feature used as a modifier ("a hair ribbon") is not the feature; near misses added to the rules' precision set.
- **The scene interview joins the injection corpus** (`SCENE_INTERVIEW`: the scene's prose as the `scene-text` block).

### Decisions

- **A column for "since", not only a flag.** Without `deepReadsDraftSince`, turning the option on did nothing for a book
  whose draft facts predated its last complete pass: it was never due, and its subjects never "changed". The draft facts
  count as new from the later of their arrival and the moment the option was turned on.
- **Draft facts join only subjects the bible speaks of.** A subject with draft facts alone could only yield draft-vs-draft
  pairs, which are never raised; paging it would pay for nothing. Each subject's draft facts are capped (its newest) so a
  long manuscript never pushes the canon out of its page.
- **The deep-found mismatch is held to the claim, not to a rule.** The reading's settlement asked whether the rules still
  found the pair; for a deep-found mismatch they never did, so it would have been settled FIXED at the scene's next read
  with the claim still standing.
- **"Work tonight" is read from rows, never from the schedule's clock.** The order of the night (check 02:23, reading
  03:07) is used only in one direction: before the night's draft dispatch has queued any run in a workspace, the scenes
  waiting are the reading's work to come. The check's work is only its live runs: a book it did not queue tonight waits
  for the next night, so it never holds the reading to half (an author saving at 02:50 would otherwise halve every night).
- **No race lets both pass half while both have work.** The total binds every call, so the two can never both be over
  half; a purpose crosses its half only at a reservation that, under the spend lock, found the other with no work.
  Work that appears afterwards (a scene saved, a run queued by a repeat dispatch) binds the next reservation.
- **"By the database's clock".** The 30-day window is computed from the database's now(); the settlement times are
  written by the jobs as before. No deletion job: rows past the window simply are not shown (the anchors a settled
  mismatch keeps are 5.3's, unchanged; their retention is the book's).
- **`settleOthersForFact` (no caller) was changed too**, so it cannot write a draft settlement the CHECK refuses.

### Limits

- A draft reading whose dispatch is late (after the scheduled 03:07) counts as "has work" only until its dispatch queues
  a run; a workspace the dispatch's fair-share bound leaves out tonight keeps the check to half while its scenes wait.
- The half is measured over the night's window like the share, so a call charged just before the window counts for
  neither.
