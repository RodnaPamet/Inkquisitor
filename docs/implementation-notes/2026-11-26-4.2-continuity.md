# 2026-11-26 — 4.2 Continuity checks and open threads (D28)

**Commit:** uncommitted (branch `wip/4.2`, cut from `main` at `86c366e`)

4.1 made every accepted bible change a source of facts and proposed a
contradiction when a new fact plainly disagreed with a live one. 4.2 turns that
record into continuity: the contradiction gains a kind (a value, whereabouts,
knowledge, a timeline) read against the book's one story order (D25), a nightly
job looks for contradictions the save-time check could not see (rules over the
whole book, then a deep-tier model check), the author resolves each one —
including "both are true", whose explanation becomes a fact — and the book gains
registries of promises, clues and open questions and a record of propagation
debt ("may be stale"), all on one Continuity page.

## Design

```
save (any writer) ── BibleEntryVersion INSERT ─┬─▶ 4.1 trigger: BibleFactExtraction (+ EmbeddingTask)
                                               └─▶ 4.2 trigger: StaleFlag upsert for every OTHER element whose
                                                   versions name (sourceAnswerIds) an ANSWERED answer to a question
                                                   about this element (its own, or a relation's with it at one end;
                                                   never aboutPending). Reorders (movedInOrder) raise nothing.

bible-facts job (4.1's one call, its output schema extended)
  shown:  the entry, its live facts, AND the book's open threads (≤ 20, oldest first)
  answer: facts + threads[] (kind, title, quote, paysOff: n of a shown thread or 0)
  code:   validateBibleThreads (quote in the entry, title grounded, no copied instruction, D14 flag) →
          StoryThreadProposal NEW_THREAD | SET_STATE, keyed once per book ever (dedupeKey)

02:23 UTC continuity-dispatch (fair-share discovery, ids only) → continuity-check per changed book
  look/claim  a book whose newest live fact is newer than coveredThrough (or a pass under way)
  sweep       StaleFlags whose entry was saved since: CLEARED (the page reads it derived anyway)
  rules       detectRuleContradictions over every live fact (bounded FACTS_READ_MAX), the story clock from the
              ranks (D25: one free order per book and kind) → StoryFactProposal basis RULE, kind
  deep        planNight: subjects in a stable order (kind:id) from passCursor; ≤ 2 calls × ≤ 100 facts,
              a subject's facts together; each call's worst case inside the ENFORCED per-call budget
              (cut, halved, else CALL_OVER_BUDGET) → per call: chargeModelCalls, kill switch, ceiling,
              D11 gate → validateContinuityPairs → proposals basis DEEP (checkRunId) → cursor saved
  finish      OK (coveredThrough = passTarget) | PAGED (cursor kept) | stops (CEILING, KILL_SWITCH, …:
              resumable, never FAILED) → audit last

Continuity page (/w/:slug/books/:bookId/continuity, bible.view; decisions bible.edit)
  contradictions (both sources)  → keep newer | keep older | both true (explanation → AUTHOR_RESOLUTION fact) | not one
  threads + proposals            → accept | reject | add/edit/state/remove by hand (undo toast)
  open questions                 → threads of kind OPEN_QUESTION + DECIDE_LATER answers still empty + unresolved contradictions
  stale entries                  → "grill me on what changed" (SELECTION + staleFlagId, depth 5) | dismiss
  nightly                        → last run, outcome, subjects covered / remaining, pass under way
```

## Decisions

- **Contradictions stay in `StoryFactProposal`.** 4.1 built the record for
  this; 4.2 adds a `kind`, the `DEEP` basis (with the run that raised it), the
  decision `INVALIDATE_NEWER` (keep the older) and a required explanation for
  `KEEP_BOTH` on a contradiction. The explanation is a `StoryFact` of kind
  `AUTHOR_RESOLUTION` (CONFIRMED, not automatic, naming the proposal and the
  author, sanitised through the fact sanitiser): the author's resolution is a
  fact like any other, which later checks and the frontier read. One pair is
  raised once, in either order (the reverse pair is checked before insert).
- **The story clock is the rank alone (D25).** `storyClock` positions every
  placed element of each ordered kind by `compareRanks`; a fact "at" an element
  inherits its place. Two orders are never compared. A text story time is a
  second signal (`textTimesOverlap`): unknown overlaps everything, so the rule
  is conservative toward proposing — the author decides.
- **Rules first, the model second.** `ruleContradiction` covers VALUE (AGE,
  OCCUPATION, PLACE_TYPE), WHEREABOUTS (LOCATION at overlapping times) and
  KNOWLEDGE (two KNOWLEDGE facts about the same proposition with opposite
  polarity when they overlap, or knowing at a point before a point of not
  knowing; not knowing and then knowing is learning). Every rule needs the same
  linked subject element. The deep check sees what the rules cannot (OTHER,
  TIMELINE). Its answer is numbers and a code, so rule 2 holds by construction;
  `validateContinuityPairs` refuses malformed items, more than
  `CONTINUITY_PAIRS_MAX`, unknown numbers, self pairs, repeats, pairs already
  proposed and facts no longer live.
- **Nightly, bounded, resumable (D28).** Two deep calls a night, a hundred facts
  each, a subject's facts in one call; the cursor is `kind:id` (no author text
  in a stored cursor; a NUL separator was refused by PostgreSQL). A thousand-fact
  book takes about five nights for its first pass, then only what changed. The
  per-call budget is $0.75, enforced: the worst case with no facts is about
  $0.60 (most of it 2.1's repair turn re-sending the output budget, 4,000 tokens)
  and a hundred facts about $0.74, so a full page fits; a night's worst case is
  $1.50 a changed book, its typical cost a tenth of that, inside the $50 ceiling.
  A stop at the ceiling, the kill switch, D11 or the rate minute keeps the cursor
  and is never FAILED. The rules run even when D11 refuses the model.
- **Registries: one table with a kind, no version table.** Promise, Clue and
  OpenQuestion share the lifecycle, the text, the isolation and the page, so one
  `StoryThread` table with `kind` and a kind-checked `state`. A thread is not a
  bible entry: its history is the audit trail (create, edit, state, remove), its
  provenance its proposal (citing the version and the words), and concurrent
  edits are refused by an optimistic `version` (409 VERSION_CONFLICT). A version
  table would duplicate the audit trail for text the author edits by hand.
- **Registries cost no call.** Threads are proposed by 4.1's existing bible fact
  extraction (its prompt and output schema extended; its record re-run), shown
  the book's open threads so it can propose "paid off" citing where. Interviews
  feed the registries through the versions they produce. A proposal is keyed
  once per book, ever (a rejected one is never proposed again).
- **Open questions are partly derived.** A DECIDE_LATER answer whose field is
  still empty, and every pending contradiction, are listed as open questions
  without being stored as threads: storing them would make a second record of
  the same fact that could disagree with the first.
- **"Cited" is conservative (D28).** A flag rests on the downstream entry's own
  accepted version naming an ANSWERED answer to a question about the upstream
  element — the element's own questions, or a relation's with it at one end; a
  question about a beat not yet created counts for nobody. Nothing transitive:
  Ilse citing Wren is flagged when Wren changes, never when Marrow (whom Wren
  cited) does. A flag is raised by a trigger in the version's transaction (a
  rolled-back save raises nothing), one row per pair, and clears when its entry
  is saved again (read: the entry's newest non-reorder version passes the flag's
  number; the nightly sweep writes CLEARED) or the author dismisses it; a later
  change re-raises a dismissed flag.
- **The grill session is SELECTION plus the flag.** It reuses 3.3's scope (one
  element) with `staleFlagId` (refused 409 STALE_FLAG_GONE unless it is that
  element's open flag), depth 5, the stale entry's fields challenged first
  (`challengeAlways`, up to `STALE_CHALLENGES_MAX`), and the upstream entry
  before and after as one fenced `upstream-change` data block (truncated by
  grapheme). `QUESTION_PROMPT` gained one instruction line, so every pack record
  was re-run.
- **Where the proposals panel lives.** The Facts page lists facts; deciding
  what contradicts what is continuity, so the panel moved to the Continuity page
  and the Facts page links there with a pending count.
- **Retention.** Every continuity row cascades with its book (and so with the
  30-day book purge); threads and proposals are encrypted at rest and sanitised;
  runs and flags hold ids, counts and codes only.

## Review fixes

- **A night's rule proposals are bounded.** Unknown story time overlaps
  everything, so one subject with a few hundred whereabouts makes tens of
  thousands of WHEREABOUTS pairs. Asked as one OR branch per pair, the held
  check ran past PostgreSQL's bind-parameter limit (and, sliced, past the
  10-second transaction); the run failed every night. Held pairs are now read
  by two id lists (`proposedPairsAmong`), and a night proposes at most
  `CONTINUITY_PROPOSALS_PER_RUN_MAX` (1,000) contradictions a book; the rest
  follow on later nights (counted `DEFERRED`), each pair once.
- **A new thread is keyed by its kind and the entry's words.** The key was the
  model's title, which is reworded from call to call, so a rejected thread came
  back under a new title.
- **NO_FACTS moves the coverage.** A book due only for facts the check cannot
  read (an unlinked subject) was queued again every night.
- **The dispatcher takes the book checked least recently first** within a
  workspace (and among workspaces in a round): books stay due while a pass is
  under way, so an order by id handed the same books every night's places.

## Owner items

- The nightly schedule (02:23 UTC) and the per-night bounds are constants; a
  real-model evaluation record for the deep check must come from the Evals
  workflow (`--continuity`) before it runs in production (D11).
- Thread proposals from interviews come only through accepted versions; a
  dedicated interview-side thread proposal was not built.
- There is no per-workspace bound on a night's deep calls: every changed book
  of a workspace gets its two calls (up to `CONTINUITY_DISPATCH_MAX` books a
  night across workspaces), so a workspace with many changed books can spend
  most of its month's ceiling in one night, ahead of its interviews.
