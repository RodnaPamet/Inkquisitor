# What Inkquisitor is

Inkquisitor helps you take a book from its first idea to the point where it is
ready to publish. It does that by asking you questions.

## The idea: the product asks, you answer

Most writing tools wait for you to write. Inkquisitor works the other way
round. You tell it a little about your book, and it asks you about the things
the book will need: the plot, the people in it, where it happens, what it is
about, how it should feel and how it should sound. You answer in your own
words, and your answers become your **book bible** — the record of everything
that is true about your book.

## Your book bible

Every part of your book has an entry in the bible: a character, a place, a
theme, the rules of your world, your style. Each entry has a button,
**Enrich**, that starts a short interview about it. When you finish, Inkquisitor
shows you what it would add to the entry, drawn from what you said, and you
accept it, change it or turn it down. Nothing reaches your bible until you
accept it, and every change you accept is kept as a new version, so you can
always see what changed and go back.

You choose how the questions come: how deep they go, how much detail they ask
for, and in what manner — a gentle mentor, a ruthless editor, a reader in your
genre.

## What Inkquisitor will not do

- **It does not write your book.** It asks, it summarises your own answers
  into your bible, and it points out what is missing or does not fit. The
  words of your manuscript are always yours.
- **It does not ask what you have already answered.** Before it asks, it reads
  your bible.
- **It does not pretend.** A readiness score over nothing reads "not assessed",
  never 100%.

## Your work stays yours

Inkquisitor is built so that your bible and your answers are private to your
workspace: encrypted when stored, never written to the product's logs and
never used for analytics. When a question is generated from your book, your text is
sent to the model provider only under terms that do not keep it.
