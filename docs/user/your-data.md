# Your work, your data

## How Inkquisitor uses AI

No AI model writes or rewrites your manuscript. Every word of your scenes is yours.

- A scene's text changes only when you type, paste, import a file you chose, accept a margin suggestion, or restore
  one of your own earlier versions.
- Margin notes and their suggestions come from fixed rules, not a model, and nothing changes until you accept one.
- A research finding reaches a scene only as a note beside it, never in its text.
- This is checked automatically: the product's build fails if any path would let a model's output reach a scene's
  text.

Inkquisitor's models ask you questions, summarise your own answers into your book bible when you accept them, and
point out what is missing or does not fit. You can read this in the product too, under **Settings → How Inkquisitor
uses AI**.

## Recently deleted scenes

When you delete a scene, it goes to your manuscript's **Recently deleted** list for 30 days. Restore it from there and
it comes back with its text, its note, its card, its links and its whole history, in its old place (or at the end of
its chapter, if another scene has taken that place). After 30 days it is deleted for good.

Deleting a whole chapter deletes its scenes at once, including any of them waiting in Recently deleted; the
confirmation tells you how many.

## Export everything

An owner of a workspace can download everything in it — every book's bible, manuscript (as a Word document and as
Markdown), interviews, sources, research, and every version and decision you made — in one archive, under
**Settings → Your data → Export everything**.

The archive is encrypted with AES-256 under a passphrase you choose when you download it. Choose one you will
remember, at least 12 characters long: Inkquisitor never keeps it and cannot recover it. Any zip tool that reads
AES-encrypted archives opens it, such as 7-Zip, WinZip, Keka or The Unarchiver. Inside, a README describes every file.

## Deleting your workspace

An owner can delete the whole workspace under **Settings → Your data → Delete workspace**, typing its name to confirm.
You can also ask for your account to be deleted with it, when it is your only workspace.

The workspace closes at once for everyone in it. For 30 days an owner can restore it, whole: sign in, and the page that
says you have no workspace lists it with a **Restore** button. After 30 days it is deleted for good, with every book,
every version, your sources and your account if you asked. A record that the deletion happened is kept, holding none
of your writing.
